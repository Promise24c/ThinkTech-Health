# Health App User Manual

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the Health App! This application is designed to help chronic patients manage their health by tracking their medications, health metrics, and checkup schedules.

## 2. Getting Started

To use the Health App, you will need to open the project in Android Studio and run it on an Android emulator or a physical device.

## 3. Login and Registration

### 3.1. Registration

If you are a new user, you will need to create an account.

1.  On the login screen, tap the "Register" link.
2.  Enter your desired username and password.
3.  Confirm your password.
4.  Tap the "Register" button.

### 3.2. Login

Once you have an account, you can log in to the app.

1.  Enter your username and password on the login screen.
2.  Tap the "Log in" button.

## 4. Main Screen

After logging in, you will see the main screen, which serves as a dashboard for the app. From here, you can access all the main features:

*   **View Medications:** See a list of your current medications.
*   **Add Medication:** Add a new medication to your list.
*   **View Health Metrics:** See a history of your recorded health metrics.
*   **Add Health Metric:** Record a new health metric reading.
*   **View Checkups:** See a list of your upcoming and past checkups.
*   **Add Checkup:** Add a new checkup to your schedule.
*   **Healthy Eating Tips:** A daily tip to help you maintain a healthy lifestyle.

## 5. Medication Management

### 5.1. Adding a Medication

1.  From the main screen, tap the "Add Medication" button.
2.  Fill in the details for your medication:
    *   **Name:** The name of the medication.
    *   **Dosage:** The dosage (e.g., "1 tablet", "10mg").
    *   **Frequency:** How often you take the medication (e.g., "Once a day").
    *   **Time:** The time(s) you take the medication (e.g., "08:00, 20:00").
    *   **Stock:** The number of pills/doses you have.
    *   **Low Stock Threshold:** The number at which you want to be warned about low stock.
3.  Tap the "Save" button.

### 5.2. Viewing Medications

1.  From the main screen, tap the "View Medications" button.
2.  You will see a list of all your medications.

## 6. Health Metrics

### 6.1. Adding a Health Metric

1.  From the main screen, tap the "Add Health Metric" button.
2.  Fill in the details for your health metric reading:
    *   **Blood Pressure:** (e.g., "120/80").
    *   **Blood Sugar:** (e.g., "90 mg/dL").
    *   **Heart Rate:** (e.g., "70 bpm").
    *   **Notes:** Any additional notes.
3.  Tap the "Save" button.

### 6.2. Viewing Health Metrics

1.  From the main screen, tap the "View Health Metrics" button.
2.  You will see a list of all your past health metric readings, with the most recent at the top.

## 7. Checkups

### 7.1. Adding a Checkup

1.  From the main screen, tap the "Add Checkup" button.
2.  Fill in the details for your checkup:
    *   **Doctor's Name:** The name of the doctor.
    *   **Location:** The location of the appointment.
    *   **Date:** The date of the appointment.
    *   **Notes:** Any additional notes.
3.  Tap the "Save" button.

### 7.2. Viewing Checkups

1.  From the main screen, tap the "View Checkups" button.
2.  You will see a list of your upcoming and past checkups, with the most recent at the top.

## 8. Notifications

The app will automatically send you a notification when it's time to take your medication, based on the times you entered when you added the medication.
